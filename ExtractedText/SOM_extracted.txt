The SLU School of Medicine finds its roots in a clinical
laboratory founded in 197], dedicated to spearheading the
region's difficulties with various health problems, also
offering programs in Pharmacy, Medical Technology, and
Nursing.

In 1976, the SLU College of Medicine was officially founded,
followed by the opening of the SLU Hospital of the Sacred
Heart in 1977.

Originally started with two departments, the schoo! now
boasts 16 departments encompassing the basic and
clinical sciences.

SLU School of Medicine has since then grown to become
one of the top Medical schools in the country, recently
placing 7 on the 2025 Physicians Licensure exam with 100
or more examinees.

APMed and MD: What's the difference?

* Accelerated Pathway for Medicine (APMed) is a 7-year
program that leads straight to the degree Doctor of
Medicine (MD) without the need of a pre-Medicine
program. This shortens the total time of medical
education by two (2) years.
