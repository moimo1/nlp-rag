School of Nursing, Allied Health, and Biological Sciences

VISION AND MISSION

The School of Nursing, Allied Health, and Biological Sciences (SONAHBS) envisions herself as the seat of excellence in Nursing, Allied Health and Biological Sciences Education that is dynamic and inspired by a Christian way of living, producing globally competitive and value-laden nurses and health-care professionals in service of humanity.

SCHOOL HISTORY
The School of Nursing, Allied Health and Biological Sciences is a reunification of two schools: the School of Natural Sciences (SNS), which was once the College of Natural Science and split off from the College of Liberal Arts in 1968; and the School of Nursing, which split off from the College of Natural Sciences in 1976.

ADMISSION INFORMATION

Undergraduate First Year Students
* All undergraduate first year applicants must take and pass the SLU College Entrance Examination (SLU-CEE) to qualify for the program of their choice.
* For specific admission announcements and updates, please visit the SLU- University Registrar's Office Facebook page via https://www.facebook.com.slu.uro.

Transfer Students
* Except for the Medical Laboratory Science and Nursing programs, SONAHBS accept transferees in all departments. All international students shall undergo review of their document and when warranted after due assessment, shall undertake the English Proficiency Test (EPT) before the QE.
