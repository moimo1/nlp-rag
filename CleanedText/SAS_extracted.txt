School of Advanced Studies

VISION AND MISSION

The School of Advanced Studies (SAS) is envisioned as a Graduate School that stands for excellence and innovation and that will be globally identified for the distinction of its programs and the quality of its research.

SCHOOL HISTORY

The biggest institution of higher learning in Northern Philippines was a one-building college, its campus hewn out of the hillside of the Baguio Cathedral when Saint Louis University had its collegiate beginnings in 1952. In 1956, the master's program in Education of the then Saint Louis College was granted government recognition. In 2018, with the growing number of Graduate Programs being offered, the School of Advanced Studies (SAS) was established, housing all the university's Graduate Programs that are geared towards advanced, interdisciplinary scholarly research and academic work. With the Dean as its overall administrator, the school is composed of seven (7) clusters namely, Accountancy, Business and Management (ABM), Computing and Information Technology (CIT), Engineering (ENGG), Liberal Arts (LIBARTS), Natural Sciences (NATSCI), Nursing (NRSG), and Teacher Education (TEACHED). To ensure efficient supervision and management of programs in each cluster, Graduate Program Coordinators (GPCs) with aligned qualifications are designated. Such a structure has existed since 1990 when Program Coordinators were appointed to assist in the smooth implementation of programs and activities at the graduate level. Launched in 2025, the Graduate School Society champions the voice of duly enrolled SAS students while leading dynamic co-curricular programs that foster holistic growth and an enriching graduate experience.

ADMISSION INFORMATION

* Applicants are accepted based on academic, personal qualifications and admission exam performance. They must also provide any special requirement that is set by the Graduate Program cluster.
* Fora Master's degree, applicants must have Baccalaureate degree from a recognized institution and should pass the qualifying exam.
* For a Doctorate degree, applicants must have Master's degree in related fields from recognized institution.
* For international students, applicants must have the following:
- One (1) original copy of the transcript of records duly authenticated by the Philippine Embassy (Apostilled) in the student's country of origin
- Photocopy of biopage of passport
- Birth certificate authenticated by the Philippine Embassy in their country
- Certificate of graduation from previous bachelor's degree or master's degree
- Documentary clearance fee
- Qualified applicants who are non Filipinos and/or graduates of international schools will be required to settle a one-time-payment of an Out-of-State-Fee
