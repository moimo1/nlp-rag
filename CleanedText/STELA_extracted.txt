School of Teacher Education and Liberal Arts

VISION AND MISSION
The School of Teacher Education and Liberal Arts (STELA) is envisioned as an institution for excellence in Teacher and Liberal Arts Education committed to the CICM mission of education of “missio et excellentia” in the formation and training of competent, creative, socially-involved professionals imbued with Christian Spirit.

SCHOOL HISTORY

The School of Teacher Education and Liberal Arts goes back to its roots from two separate Schools from Saint Louis University. The School of Teacher Education which houses the Professional Education Programs while the School of Humanities is home of the Liberal Arts programs. In academic year 2016-2017 and under the visionary leadership of Rev. Fr. Gilbert B. Sales, CICM, both schools have been strategically merged to intensify the learning experience of students and aim to promote camaraderie within the Louisian Community.

ADMISSION INFORMATION

Undergraduate First Year Students
* All undergraduate first-year applicants must pass the SLU College Entrance Examination (SLU-CEE) to qualify for the program of their choice.
* For specific admission announcements and updates, please visit the SLU-University Registrar's Office Facebook page via https://www.facebook.com/slu.uro.

Transfer Students
* SLU admits transferees in STELA program subject to their compliance with pertinent requirements and guidelines. Transfer applicants must undergo a Qualifying Examination (QE) and if qualified, will take the Personality Test and go through an Interview. All international students shal undergo review of their documents and when warranted after due assessment, shall under- take the English Proficiency Test (EPT) before the QE.
